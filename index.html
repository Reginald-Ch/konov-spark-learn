What KONOV Teaches

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robotics, Python coding, App Development, Prompt Engineering, Data Literacy and Creative emerging tech — all designed for African learners ages 6 to 16.

Core Programs in Accra, Ghana

  • AI & ML Workshops — weekend and after-school sessions for schools and community groups in Accra and across Ghana.
  • Robotics & Coding Classes — hands-on builds where kids program robots, train models and ship real projects.
  • Summer Tech Camp Ghana — an immersive multi-week camp where kids build AI-powered apps, games and hardware.
  • Youth Hackathons & Tech Fairs — FORGE Studio hackathons with mentors, judges and prizes for young builders.
  • App Development Studio — teens learn to design and publish real web and mobile apps.
  • MeAI App — a comic-style, project-based AI learning app kids use at home (meaitech.com).

Why Parents & Schools in Ghana Choose KONOV

Most edtech is designed for the West. KONOV is built for African classrooms, homes and internet speeds — with authentic African mentors, real projects kids can show off, and a curriculum spanning AI, robotics, coding, hackathons and tech. We partner with schools across Accra and Ghana to bring practical AI & ML learning into every classroom.
