What KONOV Technologies Teaches

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, chatbot and conversational AI building, data literacy, prompt thinking, and creative problem-solving — all designed for young African learners ages 6 to 18.

Programs in Accra, Ghana

  • AI Foundations (Ages 6-16) — a beginner-friendly program covering AI basics, data literacy, machine learning, and prompt thinking.
  • Me AI Creators (Ages 6-18) — learners use Me AI to build chatbots, train models, and complete hands-on AI projects.
  • School AI Workshops — short, practical AI learning experiences for schools that want students to explore artificial intelligence.
  • Tertiary AI Innovation Hub — applied AI programs for tertiary students focused on AI tools, product building, automation, and real-world innovation.
  • Youth AI Hackathons — FORGE Studio hackathons with mentors, judges and prizes for young AI builders.
  • Summer AI & Tech Camp — vacation programs where learners explore AI, coding, and creativity through hands-on projects.
  • Me AI — KONOV's AI creation platform for learners ages 6-18 (meaitech.com).

Why Parents & Schools in Ghana Choose KONOV Technologies

KONOV Technologies is AI-first, practical, and built for African learners. We don't only teach students how to use technology — we help them understand how intelligent systems work and give them the tools to create their own. We partner with schools across Accra and Ghana to bring practical AI education into every classroom.
