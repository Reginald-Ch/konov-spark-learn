What We Do

We teach kids how intelligent systems actually think — from prompt design and model training to building real AI-powered apps and robotics projects. Our programs are designed to be joyful, rigorous, and unmistakably African.

Core Programs

  • AI & ML Workshops — weekend and after-school sessions for schools and community groups.
  • Summer Tech Camp — an immersive multi-week camp where kids ship real AI projects.
  • Tech Fairs & Hackathons — youth-friendly build events with mentors and prizes.
  • MeAI App — comic-style, project-based AI lessons kids can use at home.

Why KONOV

Most edtech is designed for the West. KONOV is built for African classrooms, homes, and internet speeds — with authentic mentors, local languages, and stories kids recognize.
